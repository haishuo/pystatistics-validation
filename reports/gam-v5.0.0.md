# Validation report — pystatistics `gam` v5.0.0

> This report validates **pystatistics gam v5.0.0**, installed from **pypi**. Evidence lives in `artifacts/gam/v5.0.0/`. It was generated by `render_report.py` from frozen artifacts — **do not edit numbers by hand**; fix the artifact or the renderer and re-render.

**Subsystem:** Generalized additive models — penalized regression splines (gam) (`pystatistics.gam`)

## 1. What statistical procedure is implemented?

gam() fits a generalized additive model g(E[y]) = Xβ + f_1(x_1) + … + f_m(x_m), where each smooth f_j is a penalized regression spline, mirroring R's mgcv::gam for common use. Smooths may be univariate s(x); tensor-product te(x,z,…)/ti(x,z,…) (a row-wise Kronecker product of marginal bases with one smoothing parameter PER MARGIN, ti removing the marginal main effects for functional-ANOVA models te(x)+te(z)+ti(x,z)); or isotropic multivariate s(x,z,…) (a single thin-plate spline of covariates on a shared scale). A univariate smooth may carry a by-variable: a CONTINUOUS by, s(x, by=z), for the varying-coefficient term z·f(x), or a FACTOR by, s(x, by=g, by_type='factor'), for a separate smooth per level of a grouping variable (mgcv's continuous and factor by=). The estimated negative-binomial dispersion is exposed on the fitted result as GAMSolution.theta. It supports the cubic-regression-spline (bs='cr', default) and thin-plate-regression-spline (bs='tp') bases, plus cyclic-cubic (cc) and P-spline (ps), usable as tensor margins; the gaussian, binomial, poisson, Gamma and negative-binomial (family='nb', with the dispersion theta estimated by profiling the REML score, mgcv's nb()) families (penalized IRLS for the non-Gaussian ones); automatic smoothing-parameter selection by GCV (mgcv GCV.Cp semantics — GCV for estimated scale, UBRE for known scale) or Laplace REML; and a user-fixed sp= to reproduce a specific fit. The solution reports the parametric coefficient table with real posterior standard errors, per-smooth and total effective degrees of freedom (EDF) and reference df, the selected smoothing parameters, the GCV/UBRE/REML score, deviance / null deviance / AIC / scale, fitted values, the Bayesian posterior covariance (mgcv's Vp), and the mgcv-style approximate significance table for the smooth terms. pystatistics matches R mgcv::gam. The GAM module is CPU-only: gam() exposes no backend= parameter.

## 2. Which algorithms are used?

- Bases (mgcv-exact construction, verified against mgcv::smoothCon to ~1e-15). cr: the cardinal natural-cubic-spline basis of Wood (2017 §5.3.1) — knots at type-7 quantiles of the unique covariate values, the banded second-derivative relations D/B, penalty S = D'B⁻¹D, and mgcv's scale.penalty normalisation S ← S / (‖S‖₁ / ‖X‖_∞²). k is the basis dimension exactly as mgcv's k. tp: the thin-plate regression spline of Wood (2003) — eigen-truncation of the |Δ|³/12 kernel with the {1, x} polynomial null space RETAINED as unpenalised columns, knots capped at 2000 unique values (mgcv max.knots; above the cap a deterministic even-index subsample replaces mgcv's RNG sample — a documented divergence). The tp parameterisation differs from mgcv's by an orthogonal reparameterisation, so its fits/EDF/function-space smoothing are identical to mgcv's but its raw coefficients and sp units are not directly comparable.
- Tensor-product & isotropic multivariate smooths (mgcv-equivalent). te(x,z,…) forms the row-wise Kronecker product of the marginal model matrices and one Kronecker-embedded penalty per margin (S^(t) = I⊗…⊗S_t⊗…⊗I), each with its own smoothing parameter; mgcv's tensor-level scale.penalty is applied to each embedded penalty against the assembled tensor design, and a single sum-to-zero constraint is absorbed into the whole tensor. ti(x,z,…) centres each margin FIRST (removing main effects) then tensors, with no overall constraint; a single-variable te(x)/ti(x) reduces to a centred 1-D smooth. Isotropic s(x,z,…) generalises the thin-plate basis to d≥2 covariates (Green's function of the Euclidean knot distances, order m=2, polynomial null space {1,x_1,…,x_d}) with one shared penalty. The unconstrained te tensor basis and its penalty list match mgcv::smoothCon(te, absorb.cons=FALSE) to ~1e-9 (cr margins); ti and isotropic s are function-space validated (their marginal centring / eigen basis differ from mgcv's by an orthogonal reparameterisation, as for the 1-D tp basis). A tensor smooth's several penalties OVERLAP (their null space is the tensor product of the marginal null spaces), so the REML penalty log-determinant log|S_λ|₊, its null-space dimension M_p and their gradient are computed JOINTLY per smooth rather than summed per penalty; ordinary (single-penalty) smooths reduce to the previous block-orthogonal formulas exactly and are numerically unchanged.
- Continuous by-variable (varying-coefficient) & negative-binomial family (VA-2 / VA-3). s(x, by=z) with a CONTINUOUS z fits the varying-coefficient term z·f(x): the cr/tp/… basis B is built as usual and each row is scaled by z (B ← diag(z)·B), and — as in mgcv — the sum-to-zero identifiability constraint is DROPPED (the by-multiplication removes the constant confound with the intercept), so the smooth keeps its full k columns. The penalty S is unchanged and mgcv's scale.penalty normalisation is applied to the by-multiplied design, so the smoothing parameter is on the SAME scale as mgcv's sp (unlike the tp/ti reporting-unit divergence): feeding mgcv's selected sp to both engines reproduces the identical fit to fp64. family='nb' with an unknown theta wraps the penalized-IRLS fit in an OUTER optimisation that, mirroring mgcv's nb(), profiles the Laplace-REML score over log θ (each θ re-selects the smoothing parameters and refits) rather than MASS::glm.nb's plain ML θ; the θ search requires method='REML' and fails loud under GCV/UBRE (the profiled UBRE score is monotone in θ → collapses to θ≈0). The estimated θ enters every reported quantity (deviance, EDF, fitted) and is surfaced on the fitted result as GAMSolution.theta / GAMParams.theta (added at 4.8.1). A FACTOR by= (s(x, by=g, by_type='factor'), added at 4.8.1) instead fits a separate smooth per level of an integer-coded grouping variable and adds the per-level group means, matching mgcv's s(x, by=factor(g)); a categorical-looking by-column with by_type unset fails loud rather than being silently treated as continuous.
- Identifiability. Every smooth absorbs the sum-to-zero constraint Σ_i f_j(x_i)=0 exactly as mgcv (smoothCon absorb.cons): with C = colsums(B), Z spans null(C') from a QR of C', and the constrained basis/penalty are BZ, Z'SZ (k → k−1 columns). Without this the constant lies in every smooth's span and the augmented design is exactly singular against the intercept — the root cause of the pre-4.6.0 defect.
- Fit (stable augmented / reduced QR, Wood 2011). Penalized IRLS solves min‖√w(z−Xβ)‖² + Σ_j λ_j β'S_jβ by QR of the augmented matrix [√w·X ; B_λ] where B_λ'B_λ = Σλ_jS_j (per-smooth eigen square roots, cached once per fit) — NOT the penalized normal equations, whose condition number is cond(X)². Reduced form: M=√w·X=Q_xR_x (via dormqr, no explicit Q), then the solve is a (p+r)×p problem independent of n; the Gaussian-identity outer loop reuses one cached (R_x,c1) so each λ evaluation is O(p³). The penalized solve uses column-pivoted QR (dgeqp3): rank deficiency (concurvity, over-specified k surviving the penalty) drops the dependent columns with a warning naming the smooths, never a silent ridge. mu is clamped inside the family domain and η bounded before the weight/working-response step, so binomial separation surfaces as R's 'fitted probabilities numerically 0 or 1' warning and a finite fit; step halving on penalized-deviance increase, and a divergent P-IRLS raises ConvergenceError.
- Inference (from the triangular factors, no normal-equations inverse). H = A⁻¹X'WX = R⁻¹R⁻ᵀ(X'WX) (pivot-aware); total_edf = tr(H), per-smooth edf_j = tr(H[block_j]), Ref.df_j = tr((2H−HH)[block_j]) (mgcv convention). Posterior covariance V_β = scale·R⁻¹R⁻ᵀ (mgcv's Vp) — the real coefficient standard errors and the parametric table's t/z tests. log|X'WX+S_λ| = 2Σlog|diag R| over the rank block.
- Smoothing-parameter selection. GCV = nD/(n−edf)² (estimated scale) or UBRE = D/n − φ + 2·edf·φ/n (known scale), following mgcv GCV.Cp; or the Laplace REML criterion of Wood (2011), V = −ℓ(β̂) + pen/(2φ) + ½[log|A/φ| − log|S_λ/φ|₊] − (M_p/2)log2π with φ=1 for fixed-dispersion families and φ profiled analytically for the Gaussian-identity model. Minimised over logλ by L-BFGS-B from an mgcv-style tr(X'X)/tr(S) start; the objective is normalised by its value at the start point so selection is invariant to the units of the response, and the finite-difference step is sized above the inner-P-IRLS noise floor. A λ sitting on a search bound with a vanishing gradient (a genuinely null smooth, λ→∞) is a converged optimum, not a failure; outer_converged reports the distinction. Free-dispersion families under REML (e.g. Gamma) fail loud — fit them with method='GCV'.
- Scale. Gaussian-identity: RSS/(n−edf); fixed-dispersion families: 1; other free-dispersion families (Gamma): the Fletcher (2012) dispersion estimator, mgcv's scale.est default. CPU-only (fp64): gam() exposes no backend=. The former GPU path was removed at 4.6.0 (finding H1).

## 3. How does it compare to R mgcv::gam (Wood)?

mgcv::gam is the canonical penalized-regression-spline GAM and the reference for every reported quantity: parametric coefficients and their standard errors, per-smooth and total EDF and Ref.df, the selected smoothing parameters, the GCV/UBRE/REML score, deviance / AIC / scale, fitted values, the posterior covariance (Vp), and the approximate smooth-term significance table. The selection METHOD is matched between engines (GCV↔GCV.Cp, REML↔REML) — never a GCV fit against a REML fit. Agreement is an honest two-tier contract: at mgcv's selected sp fed to both engines (cr smooths, whose penalty scaling is shared) the fitted values, coefficients, EDF, scale and posterior SEs are near machine precision; under free selection the selected sp, EDF and the criterion score are bounded by the outer optimizer tolerance (mgcv's exact-derivative Newton vs pystatistics' finite-difference L-BFGS-B). Basis construction is verified directly against mgcv::smoothCon.

## 4. What numerical tolerances are expected?

- **tier1_fixed_sp_tight:** At mgcv's selected sp fed to BOTH engines (cr designs, whose penalty scaling is shared), agreement is near machine precision: fitted values ≤ ~1e-9 (≤ ~1e-13 for pure-Gaussian cases), total EDF ≤ ~1e-7, parametric coefficients ≤ ~1e-7, posterior standard errors ≤ ~5e-7 relative, and the scale estimate to fp64 round-off. Conditional on a shared sp the penalized fit is a single stable-QR solve, so the two engines agree to the arithmetic, not the optimizer.
- **tier2_free_selection_optimizer_tier:** Under free selection each engine picks its own sp. Total EDF agrees with mgcv to ≤ ~2e-3, the GCV/UBRE/REML score to ≤ ~1e-6 relative (typically ~1e-11), and fitted values to ≤ ~2e-4. For cr smooths the selected sp is directly comparable and agrees to ~1e-5 relative on identified (non-null) smooths. This tier is bounded by the outer optimizer: mgcv uses exact-derivative Newton, pystatistics finite-difference L-BFGS-B — a stated contract, not a defect.
- **null_term_sp_unidentified:** For a genuinely null or near-linear smooth the optimum is λ→∞ (EDF at its null-space floor ≈ 1), where λ is only weakly identified: pystatistics and mgcv can report smoothing parameters differing by many orders of magnitude (e.g. 1.3e8 vs 1.06e13 on the gamSim x3 null term) while the EDF agrees to ≤ 1e-4 and the fitted values are identical. sp agreement is therefore reported PER SMOOTH and is expected to be loose exactly on the terms the penalty has driven to their null space; EDF and fitted values are the meaningful comparison there.
- **tp_function_space_only:** The tp basis is function-space identical to mgcv's (hat-matrix agreement ~4e-17 at fixed λ; free-selection fitted values within ~7e-7 and EDF/GCV to full displayed precision on mcycle) but uses a different (orthogonal-reparameterisation) coordinate system, so its raw coefficients and its sp units are NOT comparable to mgcv's. tp is validated via fitted values, EDF and the criterion score, not via sp or the fixed-sp tight tier.
- **scale_invariance:** GCV/UBRE selection is invariant to the units of the response: the same data as y and c·y selects a total EDF agreeing to ≤ 1e-6 (verified across c ∈ {1e-3, 1e-2, 1, 1e6}), matching mgcv's invariance. (The pre-4.6.0 optimizer keyed its termination to the absolute criterion magnitude and silently mis-smoothed small-magnitude responses — finding H2.)
- **families_r10:** Gaussian (GCV + REML), Poisson (UBRE + REML), Binomial and Gamma (inverse and log links) GAMs all match mgcv on the two-tier contract. Poisson/binomial use UBRE selection (mgcv GCV.Cp for known scale); Gamma's dispersion uses the Fletcher (2012) estimator (matches mgcv sig2 exactly on the validated case). REML for free-dispersion Gamma fails loud (method='GCV' is the supported path).
- **hardcases_r10:** The adversarial grid matches mgcv's behaviour: a near-linear truth (λ→∞, EDF→~1), a wiggly low-noise signal (small λ, EDF near k−1), two strongly correlated smooths (concurvity — the GAM analogue of collinearity; EDF/fit/coefficients agree to ≤ 1e-14, only the approximate smooth-term statistic diverges, see limitations), a deliberately over-specified k the penalty tames, and a parametric covariate + smooth. Rank-deficient designs are detected by column-pivoted QR and warned about (not silently ridged); k larger than the number of unique covariate values is rejected up front (mgcv parity).
- **tensor_multivariate_va1:** Tensor-product and isotropic multivariate smooths match mgcv::gam under both GCV and REML. Basis: the unconstrained te(x,z) tensor basis matrix and each penalty in its S list match mgcv::smoothCon(absorb.cons=FALSE) to ≤1e-9 (cr margins). Full fits vs mgcv::gam: total EDF to ≤1e-3, scale to fp64 round-off, fitted values to ≤1e-4, and the per-margin smoothing parameters to the optimizer tier — for te(x,z) both margins' sp match mgcv (e.g. GCV [0.925,0.346], REML [0.709,0.078]) to 3 decimals; for ti(x)+ti(z)+ti(x,z) all four sp and the joint EDF match; the mixed cyclic×cubic te(bs=['cc','cr']) margin case matches to the same tolerances. Isotropic s(x,z,k=20) matches EDF/scale/fitted to machine precision (function-space, single penalty). GLM tensors validated on a Poisson te(x,z): EDF and fitted match mgcv (a margin driven to its null space sits on the λ→∞ plateau where sp is unidentified — the identified margin's sp still matches, and EDF/fitted pin the fit). This tier exercises the JOINT multi-λ REML/GCV gradient (verified against finite differences of the penalty log-determinant in isolation, and end-to-end by the per-margin sp agreement).
- **by_continuous_va2:** Continuous by-variable s(x, by=z) matches mgcv::gam under both tiers and is mgcv-EXACT. TIER 1 (mgcv's selected sp fed to both engines): fitted values, coefficients, total EDF, scale and posterior SE agree to fp64 arithmetic (≤~1e-12; ~1e-15 for the pure-Gaussian cr cases) — the by-multiplied cr basis + penalty is a single stable-QR solve. This holds for a by-variable with NEGATIVE values and a zero-variance stretch, and for a LARGE-magnitude by-column (z~N(1000,200)). TIER 2 (free REML): the selected sp is DIRECTLY comparable to mgcv's (max relative disagreement ≤~1e-6 across every case, including the large-magnitude by-column — there is NO sp-scale reparameterisation for by=, unlike tp/ti), the REML score is bit-identical, and total/per-smooth EDF, deviance and fitted values agree to the optimizer tier (≤~1e-6). A second ordinary smooth alongside the by= term, s(x,by=z)+s(w), matches on both penalties. (An earlier ad-hoc probe reporting py lambda 4.75 vs mgcv sp 0.0123 did NOT reproduce under any controlled continuous-by design and was a measurement artifact, not an estimator/parametrization gap.)
- **nb_negative_binomial_va3:** family='nb' (negative-binomial GAM with estimated dispersion θ) matches mgcv::gam(family=nb(), method='REML') under free REML. The estimated θ agrees with mgcv's m$family$getTheta(TRUE) to ≤~1.3e-5 relative across a moderate θ≈3, a large θ near the Poisson limit (py 25.837 vs mgcv 25.836), and small counts / low mean (py 3.187 vs mgcv 3.187); total & per-smooth EDF, deviance, REML score and fitted values agree to the optimizer tier (EDF ≤~1e-6, fitted ≤~1e-7). θ is estimated by profiling the Laplace-REML score over log θ (mgcv's nb() convention), NOT MASS::glm.nb's plain-ML θ. family='nb' with method='GCV' fails loud (the profiled UBRE score is degenerate in θ). The estimated θ is read from the public accessor GAMSolution.theta / GAMParams.theta (added at 4.8.1, resolving VA3-F1) and cross-checked against a deviance-inversion recovery (a strictly monotone, unique inversion) — both agree.
- **factor_by_va2:** Factor by= smooths (s(x, by=g, by_type='factor'), added at 4.8.1) match mgcv::gam(y ~ s(x, by=factor(g), bs=…) + factor(g)) under free REML. A separate smooth is fit per level of the integer-coded grouping variable and the per-level group means are added automatically, so the coefficient count is identical to mgcv (e.g. 30 for a 3-level factor at k=10) and the per-level EDF match; total EDF, deviance, REML score and fitted values agree to the optimizer tier (~1e-5..1e-7) on both the tp basis (mgcv's factor-by default) and the cr basis, with each level carrying its own smoothing parameter. Before 4.8.1 a categorical by-column was silently taken as a single continuous varying coefficient (finding VA2-F1); it now fits per-level with by_type='factor', and a categorical-looking by-column with by_type unset fails loud.
- **default_invocation_r15:** The bare default a naive user writes — gam(y, smooths=[s('times')]) on mcycle (cr basis, k=10, GCV) — matches mgcv gam(accel~s(times,bs='cr',k=10), method='GCV.Cp'): total EDF to 2e-5, GCV score to 1e-11, fitted values to 1e-4, selected sp to 5e-5. Note the DEFAULT BASIS differs across engines — pystatistics s() defaults to 'cr', mgcv s() defaults to 'tp' — so a naive port must set the basis explicitly to match; a documented porting caveat, not a correctness gap.
- **fidelity_a6:** Fail-loud, no silent substitution (RIGOR A6 / G2): an exotic basis bs='re' (the re/ds/gp/fs family is a declared scope carve-out — cc and ps ARE now implemented and validated in the correctness grid), family='quasipoisson', REML with a free-dispersion Gamma, and k > unique-x all raise ValidationError; the removed GPU backend= raises TypeError; a names= vector of the wrong length raises rather than mislabelling smooth coefficients as parametric terms (finding H3). No basis, k, family or selection method is ever silently swapped.
- **cpu_speed_g3:** CPU speed (RIGOR priority 3 / G3), same estimator, CPU-vs-CPU vs mgcv's compiled C. Single-smooth GAMs are at parity across n ∈ {200 … 50000}, near-linear empirical complexity. Multi-smooth Gaussian fits no longer lag increasingly with the number of smooths: at 4.6.1 the Gaussian smoothing-parameter search uses the exact analytic gradient of the GCV/REML criterion (one inner penalized-IRLS fit per outer step) instead of finite differences (2m+1 fits per step; finding H4), so the py/mgcv ratio is now roughly flat across smooth count (~2.0–2.7× at 1→6 smooths, n=2000) where at 4.6.0 it climbed to ~5.1× at 6 smooths. Estimates are unchanged — the search lands on the same optimum (selected sp within ~1e-3 relative, total EDF within ~2e-4 of the finite-difference path). GLM families (Poisson/binomial UBRE, non-Gaussian REML) keep the finite-difference path, their IRLS weights depending on the coefficients.
- **no_gpu_path:** The GAM module is CPU-only and exposes no backend=. A dedicated CUDA-first GPU-feasibility investigation found no shippable win: the typical regime (n ≲ 10k, p 30–80) has sub-millisecond inner evaluations with nothing to accelerate, and the only safe large-n formulations (augmented-QR ~3×, batched fp64 multi-λ ~5× at n ≥ 100k) require machinery the library does not have. The shipped-and-removed fp32 GPU path was proven silently wrong (finding H1); fp64 GPU measured slower than the CPU at typical p on the available hardware.

## Constitutional compliance & GPU backend reconciliation

R4 constitutional-compliance note for the gam public surface (gam, s, SmoothTerm, GAMSolution, GAMParams, SmoothInfo). All error types conform to CONVENTIONS.md: invalid family/method/basis, k out of range, k > unique-x, mismatched lengths, wrong-length names, and REML-on-Gamma raise core.exceptions.ValidationError; a divergent P-IRLS raises core.exceptions.ConvergenceError. The solution reports z-values for fixed-dispersion families and t-values otherwise, and labels the smooth statistic Chi.sq (known scale) or F (estimated scale), matching mgcv::summary.gam. SmoothTerm is a pure immutable spec (no per-fit mutable caches). No selector or error-type deviations found.

**GPU backend reconciliation.** gam() exposes NO backend= — the GAM module is CPU-only, following the convention that a module with no algorithmically-warranted GPU path exposes no backend parameter (not a forked ValidationError on the values). This was decided by a dedicated CUDA-first GPU-feasibility investigation (handoffs/gam-gpu-investigation.md): the fp32 path shipped through 4.5.7 was proven silently wrong (the CF-1 normal-equations band — 33.6-DOF EDF error on CUDA, a wiggly n=1000 fit silently over-smoothed from EDF 24.1 to 3.0 on MPS) with no gate, and the fp64 GPU variant measured slower than the CPU at typical p; both were removed at 4.6.0.

**Findings:**

- **H1 — fp32 GPU path returned silently-wrong smoothing selection (CF-1); GPU backend removed** _(RESOLVED at 4.6.0)_: The backend='gpu' fp32 path formed X'WX in single precision; in the small-λ regime the λ-optimizer probes by design this corrupted the EDF trace (proven on CUDA: 33.6 effective-DOF error, a NEGATIVE EDF at n=100k; reproduced on Apple MPS: a wiggly n=1000/k=40 fit silently over-smoothed from EDF 24.1 to 3.0, fitted values off by 0.50 on a unit-amplitude signal, converged=True, no gate). EDF drives GCV/REML, so this was a silently-wrong fit on a public backend. The fp64 GPU variant shared the defective normal-equations formulation and measured 0.67× the CPU at p=50. Both were removed — gam() is CPU-only and exposes no backend=. See handoffs/gam-gpu-investigation.md.
- **H0 — automatic smoothing selection was silently wrong on the default path (unconstrained singular design → garbage EDF)** _(RESOLVED at 4.6.0)_: This validation's first head-to-head found the SHOWSTOPPER: pre-4.6.0 smooth bases carried no identifiability constraint, so the constant lay in every smooth's span and the augmented design was exactly singular against the intercept (cond(X'WX)~2e17). The normal-equations EDF was garbage (erratic 0.45–13.33 against a true monotone 13→4.5 on mcycle; total_edf=−3.12 at k=10), which corrupted GCV/REML selection → the default gam() call over- or under-smoothed arbitrarily (overfit mcycle EDF 11.9 vs mgcv 8.4; over-smoothed a 4-smooth gamSim fit to near-flat, deviance 2889 vs 1512), silently with converged=True. The full numerical rewrite (mgcv-exact constrained bases + stable augmented-QR + Laplace REML + real posterior SEs) fixes it; free-selection fits now match mgcv 1.9-3.
- **H2 — GCV smoothing selection was not invariant to the units of the response** _(RESOLVED at 4.6.0 (during the rewrite's adversarial review))_: The outer optimizer's termination tests keyed on the absolute criterion magnitude (GCV scales as c² under y→cy), so the same data in different units silently selected different smoothness (EDF 4.00/2.19/3.77 for y×{1,0.01,1e6}; mgcv invariant at 3.77). Fixed by normalising the objective at the start point and sizing the finite-difference step above the P-IRLS noise floor; selected EDF is now invariant across scales and equals mgcv's. Also un-stalled flat surfaces (Gamma log-link GCV now matches mgcv to 7 digits).
- **H3 — wrong-length names silently mislabelled smooth coefficients as parametric terms** _(RESOLVED at 4.6.0 (adversarial review))_: gam() forwarded names= to the summary without checking its length against the parametric column count, so an over-long names list printed smooth basis coefficients (with their SE/z/p) under user-supplied parametric names, and a short list dropped genuine parametric rows — silently. Now raises ValidationError when len(names) != parametric columns.
- **VA2-F1 — factor-by was silently misinterpreted as a continuous by-variable (fail-loud gap)** _(RESOLVED at 4.8.1)_: s(x, by=z) fits the CONTINUOUS varying-coefficient term z·f(x) by multiplying the basis rows by the numeric column z; this surface is and remains mgcv-EXACT (VA-2 tier-1 ~1e-12, tier-2 sp directly comparable ≤~1e-6). At 4.8.0 there was no factor/categorical by-variable path and no guard: handed a 3-level factor coded {0,1,2} as the by-column, pystatistics silently multiplied the basis by the integer code and fit ONE varying-coefficient smooth (converged=True, no warning) where mgcv's s(x, by=factor(g)) builds a smooth per level — a silent misinterpretation of the common mgcv idiom s(x, by=group). Fixed at 4.8.1 two ways: (a) s(x, by=g, by_type='factor') now fits a separate smooth per level (adding the per-level group means), matching mgcv's s(x, by=factor(g)) + factor(g) — identical coefficient count and per-level EDF, total EDF/deviance/fitted to the optimizer tier (~1e-5..1e-7) on both tp and cr bases; (b) a categorical-looking by-column (integer-valued, low-cardinality, contiguous coding) with by_type UNSET now raises, asking the user to pick by_type='factor' or 'continuous'. Continuous by= is numerically unchanged. Re-validated at 4.8.1 (studies va2_factor_by, va2_va3_fidelity).
- **VA3-F1 — estimated negative-binomial theta had no public accessor (completeness gap)** _(RESOLVED at 4.8.1)_: family='nb' estimates the dispersion θ by profiling the Laplace-REML score (mgcv's nb() convention) and the estimate is CORRECT — it agrees with mgcv's getTheta(TRUE) to ≤~1.3e-5 relative across moderate/large/small-count regimes, and EDF/deviance/fitted match. At 4.8.0 the fitted GAMSolution/GAMParams exposed θ nowhere (family_name was the bare string 'negative.binomial' and the estimate was discarded), so the defining output of an NB model was unreadable through the public API — the validation had to recover it by inverting the deviance. Fixed at 4.8.1: GAMSolution.theta / GAMParams.theta now return the estimated θ for an auto-fit family='nb', the supplied value for a fixed NegativeBinomial(theta=...), and None for every other family. Re-validated at 4.8.1 reading θ from the accessor (study va3_nb, theta_accessor=yes).
- **H4 — multi-smooth fits slower than mgcv (finite-difference sp search)** _(RESOLVED for Gaussian at 4.6.1 (analytic gradient); GLM families keep FD (documented))_: At 4.6.0 the finite-difference smoothing-parameter search cost (2m+1) inner fits per outer step, so multi-smooth Gaussian fits lagged mgcv increasingly (~5.1× at 6 smooths, n=2000). Fixed at 4.6.1 (the mixed A.3 pattern): the Gaussian-identity path now feeds L-BFGS-B the EXACT analytic gradient of the GCV/REML criterion (dedf/dρ_j=−λ_j·tr(A⁻¹S_jH), dD/dρ_j=2λ_j·(A⁻¹S_λβ̂)'(S_jβ̂), envelope d(D+pen)/dρ_j=λ_jβ̂'S_jβ̂) — one inner penalized-IRLS fit per outer step. The py/mgcv ratio is now roughly flat across smooth count (~2.0–2.7× at 1→6 smooths) instead of climbing to ~5.1×; on the same machine the before/after is ~2.0× at 4 smooths and ~3.2× at 6. Estimates are UNCHANGED (same optimum: selected sp within ~1e-3 relative, total EDF within ~2e-4 of the FD path; analytic gradient matches central finite differences to ~1e-6). GLM families (Poisson/binomial UBRE, non-Gaussian REML) keep the finite-difference path — their IRLS weights depend on the coefficients, so the constant-weight closed form does not apply (the full Wood-2011 implicit-derivative form is deferred). A gathered non-showstopper, now resolved for the common Gaussian case.

## 6. What are the known limitations?

- Multi-smooth GENERALIZED (non-Gaussian) GAMs are still slower than mgcv, growing with the number of smoothing parameters, because the Poisson/binomial (UBRE) and non-Gaussian REML paths keep the finite-difference smoothing-parameter search ((2m+1) inner fits per outer step) versus mgcv's exact-derivative Newton — their IRLS weights depend on the coefficients, so the analytic gradient shipped at 4.6.1 does not cover them. GAUSSIAN multi-smooth fits are fixed at 4.6.1 (finding H4): the analytic GCV/REML gradient makes the py/mgcv ratio roughly flat across smooth count (~2.0–2.7×) instead of climbing to ~5.1× at 6 smooths, with estimates unchanged. Single-smooth fits are at parity across families. Correctness is unaffected throughout.
- The approximate smooth-term significance statistic (summary's F / Chi.sq column) follows the shape of mgcv's Wood (2013) test but is a simplified form; it agrees with mgcv to ~0.2% on ordinary fits but can diverge under strong concurvity (up to ~50% on the deliberately near-collinear two-smooth case, where the covariance blocks are near-singular). EDF, sp, fitted values, coefficients and scale on that same case agree to ~1e-14; only the displayed test statistic is affected. p-values from both engines are approximate (neither fully accounts for smoothing-parameter selection). A gathered, documented non-showstopper.
- AIC uses the classical GAM convention df = total_edf (+1 when the scale is estimated). mgcv ≥ 1.8 corrects df for smoothing-parameter uncertainty (Wood, Pya & Säfken 2016, 'edf2'), so mgcv's AIC() is systematically slightly larger (observed Δ ~0.1–1.1 on the validated cases). Documented on GAMParams.aic; a convention difference, not an error.
- The tp basis differs from mgcv above the 2000-unique-value knot cap: mgcv subsamples knots with its internal RNG, pystatistics takes a deterministic even-index subsample of the sorted unique values. Below the cap the fits are function-space identical to mgcv; above it they diverge by the knot choice (a documented, bounded difference). tp raw coefficients and sp units are never comparable to mgcv's (orthogonal reparameterisation) — compare tp fits via fitted values / EDF.
- method='REML' supports the fixed-dispersion families and the Gaussian-identity model; REML for a free-dispersion family other than Gaussian (e.g. Gamma) raises ValidationError (mgcv estimates their scale inside REML; pystatistics does not) — use method='GCV'. A fail-loud scope limitation.
- The GAM module is CPU-only and exposes no backend= (a deliberate, documented decision from a CUDA-first GPU-feasibility investigation: no shippable GPU win exists in the regime GAMs are used). The prior fp32 GPU path was removed at 4.6.0 after it was proven to return silently-wrong smoothing selections.
