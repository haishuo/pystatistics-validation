## 6. Findings ledger — five R-convention divergences, ALL FIXED

First-time validation of these modules surfaced five divergences from R at **4.6.10**. Per RIGOR R18 these were gathered and **fixed together** in the **4.6.11** patch — *documented is not fixed*; the bless is on the version that fixes them. No test statistic or default p-value on valid input was ever wrong (all matched R to machine precision at 4.6.10); the five were confidence-interval, warning, diagnostic, and invalid-input conventions.

| id | fn | divergence (4.6.10) | fix (4.6.11) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| F1 | `wilcox_test` | HL confidence interval used empirical percentiles of the Walsh averages (wrong endpoints for small n) while labelled "exact". | Exact order-statistic inversion (qsignrank/qwilcox) + tie-corrected uniroot; zeros dropped for signed-rank. New module _wilcox_ci.py. |
| F2 | `prop_test` | 2-sample CI always used the full 0.5 continuity correction (interval wider than R when proportions are close). | Cap the correction at min(0.5, |Δ|/Σ(1/n)), matching R. |
| F3 | `ks_test` | one-sample KS handled ties silently; R warns "ties should not be present". | Emit R's one-sample KS ties warning. |
| F4 | `anova_rm` | Mauchly p used only the first-order chi-square, omitting R's second-order (Box) term (~1e-3 off). | Add R's second-order correction: p = Pr1 + w2·(Pr2−Pr1), Pr2 at df+4. |
| F5 | `quantile` | probs outside [0,1] were silently clamped to min/max instead of raising (fail-loud gap). | Raise ValidationError on probs ∉ [0,1], matching R. |


**Showstoppers (R16):** none. All five re-validated against R at 4.6.11: F1 exact rank-sum/signed-rank CIs machine-exact vs R; approx CIs within R's uniroot tol (~1e-4); F2 CI matches R to machine precision; F3 warning present, matches R; F4 Mauchly p matches afex to machine precision; F5 raises; matches R's error.
