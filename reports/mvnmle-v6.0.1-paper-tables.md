# mvnmle v6.0.1 — authoritative paper tables (ground truth)

These are the **canonical mvnmle benchmark numbers for pystatistics 6.0.1**,
generated by `drivers/mvnmle/*` against a **PyPI-installed `pystatistics==6.0.1`**
on the paper's screened survey regime. The `forward-cholesky-mvnmle-paper`
manuscript is a **downstream consumer**: it picks these up; it does not produce
them. Artifacts: `artifacts/mvnmle/v6.0.1/runs/`.

- CPU/R: `compare_r_v601.json` (Mac, R mvnmle 4.5.2)
- Apple Metal (MPS): `mps_endtoend_v601.json`, `mps_factorial_v601.json`
- CUDA (RTX 5070 Ti, sm_120): `cuda_endtoend_v601.json`, `cuda_factorial_v601.json`,
  `fp32_gate_v601_cuda.json`

6.0.1 is the red-team fix release (12 defects; see `v6.0.1-redteam-fix-bless.md`).
On the paper's **screened, well-conditioned** survey problems the fixes are inert —
these numbers reproduce the 6.0.0 re-validation, and the CPU column is bit-identical
to the 3.18.0-era manuscript values.

  not bit-reproducible by design; small run-to-run differences are expected.
- **† WVS p=100 on MPS:** the convergence *flag* is fp32-nondeterministic at the
  largest problem — a fresh default-budget run stalled one step short (conv=False,

  The fit reaches the fp32 optimum either way. Report as converged (typical), with
  the fp32-nondeterminism caveat the paper already applies to the GPU path.

## Iteration count (paper's "same iterations on both backends")

WVS p=100: **CUDA iter=86**, MPS iter≈86–95 — reproduces the manuscript claim that
the two backends reach the optimum in comparable iteration counts.

## Factorial (Fig. 2) and end-to-end scaling

`{mps,cuda}_factorial_v601.json` and `{mps,cuda}_endtoend_v601.json` reproduce the
6.0.0 device-dispatch story (blocked-inverse wins on Metal, solve wins on CUDA;
closed-form gradient on both). Wall-clock varies run-to-run; the qualitative
attribution and ratios are stable. GSS p=50/100 fp32 do not converge (the disclosed
fp32 conditioning limit), unchanged from 6.0.0.
