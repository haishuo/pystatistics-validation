# Validation report — pystatistics `mvnmle` v3.18.0

> This report validates **pystatistics mvnmle v3.18.0**, installed from **pypi**. Evidence lives in `artifacts/mvnmle/v3.18.0/`. It was generated by `render_report.py` from frozen artifacts — **do not edit numbers by hand**; fix the artifact or the renderer and re-render.

**Subsystem:** Multivariate-normal MLE with incomplete data (`pystatistics.mvnmle`)

## 1. What statistical procedure is implemented?

Maximum-likelihood estimation of the mean vector mu and covariance matrix Sigma of a multivariate normal distribution from data with arbitrary item-level missingness (missing-at-random). Each observation contributes the marginal log-likelihood over its observed coordinates; the estimator maximizes the summed observed-data log-likelihood.

## 2. Which algorithms are used?

- Forward-Cholesky parameterization of Sigma: the optimizer works in the unconstrained space of a Cholesky factor, so every iterate is positive-definite by construction (no projection/repair step). This is the contribution the mvnmle paper formalizes.
- CPU default since 3.17.0: a fast forward-Cholesky estimator in fp64 (deterministic).
- Apple-Metal (MPS) optimizer loop since 3.18.0: a single fused objective/gradient pass per iteration; GPU reductions are fp32 (mildly non-deterministic in the last digits).
- CUDA backend (fp32) for NVIDIA hardware.
- Trace-term evaluation has two backends (direct solve vs blocked) selected per device; gradients via analytic form or autodiff (see the factorial study).

## 3. How does it compare to R mvnmle?

The CRAN mvnmle package, which maximizes the same observed-data likelihood using an inverse-Cholesky parameterization in fp64. It is the established reference implementation; agreement in loglik and in the estimated mu/Sigma summaries (Frobenius norm, log-determinant) is the correctness claim.

Also compared against: semopy (Python SEM, on the GSS survey).

## 4. What numerical tolerances are expected?

- **loglik_vs_r:** Agreement to fp64 round-off on CPU; GPU fp32 paths agree to ~1e-4 relative on loglik and may differ in the last reported digit of mu/Sigma.
- **estimate_agreement:** Cross-engine agreement checked via sigma_fro and sigma_logdet rather than full elementwise equality at large p (the full p x p matrix is O(p^2) and is only stored for p <= 100).
- **gpu_nondeterminism:** fp32 GPU reductions are not bit-reproducible across runs; timings are reported as medians over repeats and estimates as the last timed repeat.

## 6. What are the known limitations?

- R mvnmle does not run past roughly p = 50 in practical time; head-to-head agreement is therefore established at p <= 25 and the high-p story is internal (CPU vs GPU).
- GPU paths are fp32; for applications needing fp64 covariance to full precision, use the CPU backend.
- Assumes missing-at-random; the estimator does not model a missingness mechanism.
- The MLE requires the observed-data covariance to be non-degenerate; near-collinear columns make the problem ill-conditioned (the paper's data curation screens |corr| near 1).
